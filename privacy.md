# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

Home Use AI Connector is a personal software project used to connect an AI assistant to an authenticated user's Pinterest account.

## Information accessed

With the user's explicit authorization through Pinterest OAuth, the application may access:

- Pinterest account information needed to identify the authenticated user
- Boards owned by the authenticated user
- Pins owned or saved by the authenticated user

The application does not access Pinterest data without the user's authorization.

## How information is used

Pinterest data is used only to provide requested personal AI-assistant features, such as:

- Searching Pins and boards
- Organizing saved content
- Analyzing saved Pins
- Providing recommendations based on the user's own saved content

## Sharing and sale of data

Pinterest data is not sold.

Pinterest data is not shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

Data may be transmitted to services required to operate the AI connector solely for the purpose of providing the requested functionality.

## Authentication

Access to Pinterest is provided through Pinterest's OAuth authorization
